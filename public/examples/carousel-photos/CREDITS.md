## Reproduce

1. Open https://lydiatools.github.io/aspectory/carousel.html?lang=en .
2. Add all three JPGs. Choose 4:5, white background, 2% border.
3. Check their order, then download the numbered PNG ZIP. Each output is 1080 x 1350 pixels. Sources are resampled to fit; source files are not overwritten.
